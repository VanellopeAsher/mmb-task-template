# Automatic evaluation

The question is fixed: measure late electrical recovery and test whether the residual deficit co-occurs with a difference in I/Pb on the same devices, with a sham arm. The submitted design is an allocation, with no experiment menu or Agent-selected objective.

## Design reference

The resource cap is a benchmark design constraint, not an estimate of actual prices or assay precision. Each complete stress/sham contrast consumes at least two devices, six power readings and two XPS assays. Therefore min(12/2, 30/6, 10/2) = 5 is an upper bound and a balanced five-per-arm cohort attains it. Count complete per-device longitudinal/terminal records in each arm and take the smaller count. There is no prescribed cohort order or partition. Four complete pairs give 32/40, five give 40/40. Extra observations are permitted within budget.

An electrical-only schedule misses iodine; film-only XPS does not identify the joint device endpoint; allocating XPS to a separate cohort cannot pair it with both time points on the same device. Early destructive XPS precludes later readings. An unbalanced plan discards joint contrast capacity under this objective. Replication here is a resource criterion, with no power claim. Randomization and common preparation are fixed protocol assumptions, not inferred from the answer. A contrast unit counts capacity in the two arms and does not imply physically paired specimens or a paired statistical test.

The 25-point evidence block uses observed recovery, residual deficits and relative I/Pb changes plus cohort/observation limits. The 20-point update block tests implications conditional on a resolved contrast and valid design. Association does not establish causation and non-detection has finite assay and time scope. The 15-point prediction block retains real 24 h held-out recovery and the original error scale.

## Identifiability boundary

The source data do not contain replicated, joint power and calibrated XPS for the same short dark-bias devices, assay variance, or costs for comparing different protocols. The film XPS uses a substantially different stress schedule. This task therefore validates a constrained experimental-design criterion, not the general expected information value or statistical power of open-ended research proposals. The forward experiments have not been performed. A more general benchmark would need replicated candidate interventions, validated assay sensitivity and realistic resource tradeoffs.
