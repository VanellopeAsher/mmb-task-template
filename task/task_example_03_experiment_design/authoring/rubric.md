# Automatic evaluation

The problem statement defines the scored observables, conditional claims, tolerance and point allocations. The verifier uses private source-backed targets and assigns the complete reward without per-answer expert review. Experts may audit the evidence and the scoring design independently.

Visible device recovery and film iodine changes are separate measurements. The experimental options are graded by whether their comparisons and readouts can address the claimed question, with conditional updates limited to the tested protocol. The experiments have no observed follow-up outcomes. The 24 h score uses actual separately held-out measurements.
