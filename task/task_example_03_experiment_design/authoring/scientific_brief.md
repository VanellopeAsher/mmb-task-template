# Scientific brief

## Original candidate
5.1 钙钛矿离子重排可逆性与化学退化。

## Task interpretation
把问题转化为一个实验设计任务：用匹配的光照/偏压应力组、暗态恢复、瞬态电学测量和化学组分表征，区分可逆离子重新分布与不可逆化学变化。

## Evidence basis
- Zhang et al. (2019), DOI 10.1021/acs.jpcc.9b04662: reversible iodide migration under positive/negative bias on a minute timescale; reported diffusion coefficient 1.3 × 10^-12 cm² s^-1 and mobility 5 × 10^-11 cm² V^-1 s^-1.
- *Bias-Dependent Dynamics of Degradation and Recovery in Perovskite Solar Cells* (2021), DOI 10.1021/acsaem.1c00588: short-circuit-aged devices fully recover after light is switched off, while open-circuit-aged devices do not recover and can show a drop-in-dark response.
- Parion & Vermang (2026), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18423433: standardized light, thermal, and combined stress data for scalable wide-bandgap perovskite cells.

The reference protocol is a benchmark design proposal, not a claim that the exact proposed sequence has already been experimentally validated.
