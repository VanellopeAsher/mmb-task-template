# Validation notes

The scoring panel uses the source workbook for DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-73269-9 (SHA-256 `f6834ffed74b179813a459cc854e3450802a098ef665947bf71bd74a0b2a2355`). Supplementary Figure 3 panels a and c are the −0.9 V and −2.7 V dark-recovery runs. Figure 3f provides before/after I/Pb ratios for control and chelated films exposed to the same combined stress. See `recompute_reverse_bias_recovery.py` for extraction. The workbook's panel b is excluded because its labelled pre/post columns do not show an initial power loss.

Recovery and chemistry are separate contrasts on different specimens. The scores quantify evidence for each measured contrast at benchmark-defined thresholds. The published endpoints do not estimate population uncertainty, permanence beyond the measurement window, or causal shares within one device. The original Prete study remains background for the OC/SC formulation.
