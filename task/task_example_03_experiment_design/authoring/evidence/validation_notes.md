# Validation notes

The current task is anchored to Prete et al.'s OC/SC T60 recovery study. The eight rows in `authoring/evidence/model_fit_parameters.csv` were checked against Table 1 of the accepted manuscript; the OC SnO2-interface fitted-defect ratios relative to fresh are 200 at T60 and 40 after 22 h, while SC returns to 1 after 22 h. These quantities are model fits, not direct defect counts. The original non-encapsulated ToF-SIMS cohort has different aging endpoints and cannot be treated as matched T60 evidence.

The attached revision improves the scientific framing but provides no observed outcomes of its newly proposed interventions. It cannot establish a uniquely correct mechanism or a binary outcome-based design score. `outcome_based_verification.md` specifies the required independent ground truth and scoring rule. The current task verifier remains a draft and is not a scientific acceptance gate.
