## Historical activation analysis (unscored)

The raw-data witness, `solution/witness/reduction_transition.py`, projects each ramp XANES spectrum onto the line joining that sample's initial He and post-reduction H₂ spectra. It interpolates normalized Au and Cu spectra on the respective edge's −10 to +30 eV grid, matches raw scan headers for **resistor** temperature, fits a monotone transition with isotonic regression, and interpolates the 50% crossing. The resulting operational T50 values in °C are Au/Au 54.95 (69 ramp scans), Cu/Cu 88.29 (79), AuCu/Au 68.09 (15), AuCu/Cu 69.88 (16), AuCu₃/Au 70.43 (28), and AuCu₃/Cu 74.49 (28). Each fresh and reduced endpoint is one already averaged export, so scan-level endpoint uncertainty is unavailable. Energy windows of −5…+20, −15…+35, and −10…+50 eV shift any reported T50 by at most 0.72 °C. These values are not the paper's MCR-ALS onset estimates or a direct thermometer reading of the catalyst bed. Their robust qualitative implications are that both bimetallic Cu transitions precede monometallic Cu and that Au and Cu transitions in each bimetallic run are close under this projection. Endpoint choice, temperature metadata, and data-quality exclusions remain sources of uncertainty. Earlier revisions scored this midpoint with a 2 °C tolerance. It is not part of the current answer.

The earlier evidence-only pilot was regenerated from the two verified source archives and their extracted directory form, with exact agreement. This removed the former paper-answer shortcut, but did not establish a mechanism-discrimination task. A unique AuCu morphology, molecular selectivity, causal rate law, and empirical human difficulty remain unverified.

## Reaction-stage checks

The reaction-stage reference uses independently annotated 130/150 °C controls. Au/TiO₂ has m/z 54 medians 3.2907→2.5870 and m/z 56 11.983→12.521; AuCu₃/TiO₂ has 13.721→10.990 and 2.5042→4.6566. AuCu has 12.7795→10.5035 and 3.746→5.68105. All three runs show the same direction. This comparison does not establish a shared elementary mechanism. The exports are uncalibrated; these cross-run levels cannot be compared as rates. Au/Au and Cu/Cu 130/150 °C XANES change ratios are 0.03254/0.03577 and 0.04119/0.04000, respectively; AuCu/Au and AuCu/Cu remain below 0.05. A mechanism requiring bulk redox of at least one tenth of the activation contrast conflicts with all these controls, but minority surface reorganization remains unresolved.

The Au-edge post-edge fingerprint calculation from the released normalized spectra gives Pearson r=0.57852 between reduced Au and reduced AuCu, versus r=0.98842, 0.98245 and 0.97745 between reduced AuCu and its 100, 130 and 150 °C reaction states, respectively. With k ranges 4–9 or 3–10 Å⁻¹ and smoothing windows 81–121 points, the first remains 0.58–0.64 and the second 0.98–0.99. The filter suppresses a smooth background and is not an EXAFS coordination fit; differences can reflect size, disorder, electronic state, alignment and alloying. It supports a distinct reduced local/electronic fingerprint and reaction-stage similarity without identifying an atomic morphology.
