# Shortcut audit

The public article contains fitted coordination numbers and a favored H-spillover interpretation, but the task's raw-data checkpoints use independently recomputed sample-specific XANES midpoints, reaction contrasts, mass-channel windows, and a post-edge fingerprint. A citation-only or paper-label answer fails these checks. The mechanism answer is deliberately narrower than the paper's proposed causal story: earlier Cu reduction is an association, and H spillover remains one of several compatible causes.

An answer that computes all numerical fields but omits either mechanism comparison or experiment receives zero. The verifier rejects incompatible predictions and unsupported unique causal claims. The experiment format permits any intervention and observable with a justified contrast between two compatible explanations. Automated scoring checks interval separation at the reported resolution. Reviewers assess whether the intervention, controls, and assumed bounds justify that contrast, including alternative causes and the interpretation of a null result.
